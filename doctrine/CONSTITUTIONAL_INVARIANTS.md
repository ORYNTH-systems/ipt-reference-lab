# IPT Constitutional Invariants

## Repository Invariants v1.0

This document defines the non-negotiable invariants of the Integrity Preservation Theory (IPT) Reference Lab.

These invariants constrain every doctrine file, specification, case, runtime module, evidence artifact, metric report, and release document in this repository.

## INV-001 — Bounded Object Invariant

Every IPT evaluation shall identify a bounded evaluative object.

An evaluation without a bounded system is invalid.

## INV-002 — Preservation Invariant

The object of evaluation is preservation.

IPT does not evaluate general excellence, preference, performance, compliance, safety, reliability, or optimization as substitute objects.

## INV-003 — Evidence Separation Invariant

Raw observables, derived indicators, and interpretive judgments shall remain separate.

No implementation may treat observation as conclusion.

## INV-004 — Evidence Sufficiency Invariant

Evidence sufficiency constrains the admissibility of any integrity state.

A score shall not override insufficient evidence.

## INV-005 — Continuity Distinction Invariant

Continuity informs integrity but is not identical to integrity.

Structural continuity, functional continuity, and evidentiary continuity shall remain distinguishable where applicable.

## INV-006 — Degradation Distinction Invariant

Degradation shall be evaluated as loss, erosion, impairment, or weakening of preservation-relevant properties.

Degradation shall not be collapsed into performance failure alone.

## INV-007 — Recoverability Distinction Invariant

Recoverability shall remain distinct from current preservation state.

A degraded system may be recoverable.

A recovered system may or may not preserve identity.

## INV-008 — Indeterminacy Invariant

Indeterminate evaluation is a valid IPT outcome.

IPT shall not force a preserved or degraded classification when evidence does not support one.

## INV-009 — Scoring Subordination Invariant

Any score, including an Integrity Preservation Score, is subordinate to evidence sufficiency, continuity analysis, boundedness, and state classification.

A scalar score is not sovereign.

## INV-010 — State Classification Invariant

Integrity state classification is downstream of bounded system definition, evidence acquisition, measurement, continuity analysis, degradation analysis, recoverability analysis, and admissibility constraints.

## INV-011 — Implementation Subordination Invariant

Implementation choices may instantiate IPT but may not redefine IPT.

A runtime may vary.

The constitutional object may not.

## INV-012 — Non-Sovereignty Invariant

IPT evaluates preservation-oriented integrity.

It does not authorize, deny, execute, regulate, diagnose, punish, approve, or prescribe downstream action.

## INV-013 — Portfolio Boundary Invariant

IPT shall remain distinct from Unified Agency Architecture, Execution Integrity Protocol, Adaptive Orchestration Management System, and Proof of Block.

Shared concerns do not imply shared constitutional objects.

## INV-014 — Auditability Invariant

Every IPT output shall remain traceable to declared bounded system assumptions, evidence inputs, measurement transformations, estimation logic, thresholds, and integrity state rules.

## INV-015 — Domain Adaptation Invariant

IPT may be instantiated across domains.

Domain-specific observables, thresholds, and estimators may vary, but constitutional distinctions shall remain stable.

## Invariant Closure Rule

A case, runtime output, JSON evidence file, metric report, or release artifact is IPT-conforming only if it preserves all invariants in this document.

Violation of any invariant invalidates the artifact as an IPT-conforming artifact.
