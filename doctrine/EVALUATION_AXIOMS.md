# IPT Evaluation Axioms

## Repository Axioms v1.0

This document defines the constitutional evaluation axioms governing every Integrity Preservation Theory (IPT) implementation.

These axioms constrain evaluation behavior independently of implementation language, scoring model, estimation function, domain, or runtime.

## AX-001 — Bounded Evaluation

Evaluation begins only after the bounded evaluative object has been constitutionally declared.

## AX-002 — Evidence Acquisition

Evidence shall enter the evaluation only through admissible observables associated with the bounded system.

## AX-003 — Observable Separation

Observables, derived indicators, and interpretive conclusions shall remain constitutionally distinct throughout evaluation.

## AX-004 — Preservation-Oriented Evaluation

The constitutional objective of evaluation is preservation of the bounded evaluative object.

No substitute objective may replace preservation.

## AX-005 — Multi-Dimensional Integrity

Integrity evaluation shall preserve the distinct constitutional roles of:

• Continuity

• Degradation

• Recoverability

• Evidence Sufficiency

These dimensions shall not collapse into one another.

## AX-006 — Evidence Sufficiency Constraint

Integrity determination shall remain constrained by evidence sufficiency.

Insufficient evidence produces an indeterminate constitutional outcome rather than a forced determination.

## AX-007 — Continuity Constraint

Continuity contributes to preservation evaluation but shall never independently determine integrity.

## AX-008 — Recoverability Constraint

Recoverability represents future preservation potential rather than present preservation state.

## AX-009 — Scoring Constraint

Scores summarize preservation support.

Scores shall never override constitutional admissibility constraints.

## AX-010 — Integrity State Constraint

Integrity state classification is downstream of every preceding evaluative stage.

No implementation may classify integrity prior to bounded system definition, evidence acquisition, preservation estimation, and admissibility evaluation.

## AX-011 — Auditability

Every integrity determination shall be reproducible from declared inputs, mappings, estimation logic, and evaluation configuration.

## AX-012 — Non-Sovereign Evaluation

Integrity evaluation terminates with a preservation determination.

No authorization, denial, execution, regulation, diagnosis, or downstream action is produced by IPT.

## AX-013 — Domain Independence

Healthcare, artificial intelligence, finance, infrastructure, identity systems, robotics, supply chains, and future domains shall all preserve identical constitutional evaluation behavior.

Only implementation-specific observables, estimators, thresholds, and mappings may differ.

## AX-014 — Constitutional Closure

Every conforming IPT implementation shall preserve the evaluation sequence established by this document.

Violation of any evaluation axiom constitutes a non-conforming implementation.
