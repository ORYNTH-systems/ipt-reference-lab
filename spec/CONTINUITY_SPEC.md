# Continuity Specification

## Integrity Preservation Theory (IPT)

### Continuity Specification v1.0

This document defines the constitutional treatment of continuity within the Integrity Preservation Theory (IPT) Reference Lab.

Continuity is a preservation-relevant evaluation dimension.

Continuity is not the constitutional object of IPT.

Continuity informs preservation-oriented integrity evaluation but never independently determines integrity.

---

# 1. Constitutional Status

Continuity contributes to integrity evaluation.

Continuity shall remain constitutionally distinct from:

• Integrity

• Evidence Sufficiency

• Degradation

• Recoverability

• Performance

• Reliability

• Safety

---

# 2. Purpose

Continuity evaluation estimates whether constitutionally relevant preservation properties remain sufficiently maintained across time.

Continuity neither guarantees nor disproves preservation.

---

# 3. Required Continuity Dimensions

Every conforming implementation shall preserve independent treatment of:

Structural Continuity

Functional Continuity

Behavioral Continuity

Evidentiary Continuity

Temporal Continuity

Implementations may introduce additional continuity dimensions provided constitutional distinctions remain preserved.

---

# 4. Structural Continuity

Structural continuity evaluates preservation of constitutionally relevant composition.

Changes in implementation alone do not imply continuity failure.

---

# 5. Functional Continuity

Functional continuity evaluates preservation of constitutionally relevant functional capability.

Temporary behavioral variation does not necessarily imply functional discontinuity.

---

# 6. Behavioral Continuity

Behavioral continuity evaluates preservation of expected externally observable behavior.

Behavior shall never be treated as the sole determinant of integrity.

---

# 7. Evidentiary Continuity

Evidentiary continuity evaluates preservation of admissible evidence supporting continuity claims.

Missing evidence shall not be interpreted as evidence of discontinuity.

---

# 8. Temporal Continuity

Continuity shall remain explicitly evaluated across declared temporal intervals.

Evaluation timestamps shall be recorded for replay and audit.

---

# 9. Continuity Constraints

Continuity shall never:

Replace integrity.

Replace evidence sufficiency.

Replace degradation evaluation.

Replace recoverability evaluation.

Replace constitutional evaluation.

---

# 10. Runtime Requirements

Every runtime evaluation shall report:

Structural Continuity

Functional Continuity

Behavioral Continuity

Evidentiary Continuity

Temporal Continuity

Continuity Summary

Audit Metadata

Implementation Version

---

# 11. Auditability

Every continuity assessment shall be reproducible from:

Declared observables

Declared mappings

Declared implementation assumptions

Declared evaluation interval

Declared implementation version

---

# 12. Constitutional Limitation

Continuity contributes to preservation-oriented integrity evaluation.

Continuity alone shall never determine integrity state.

---

# Specification Closure

Every conforming Integrity Preservation Theory implementation shall preserve the constitutional treatment of continuity established by this specification.

Violation of these requirements constitutes a non-conforming implementation.
